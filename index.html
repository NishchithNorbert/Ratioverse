Numbers are boring.
Stories about money are not.

Every ratio is a question someone once asked about a business. Learn to ask them yourself — and you'll never look at an annual report the same way again.

Two coffee shops in Kochi each earn ₹10 lakh profit a year.

One just sold for ₹1 crore. The other sold for ₹3 crore.

Same profit. Triple the price. Why would anyone pay that?

You just discovered the P/E ratio. Investors don't pay for today's profit — they pay for expectations of future profit. Shop one trades at 10× earnings; shop two at 30×. Maybe shop two has a viral brand, three new outlets opening, a franchising deal. The price of a business is a bet on its story. +25 XP
